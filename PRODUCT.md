## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated in the brief, which recommended Astro: Astro 7 (static output), TypeScript, plain CSS,
and one small client module for the hero artwork. No backend, CMS, analytics, auth, or
contact-form service.

## Users

Technical hiring managers, engineers, and research collaborators evaluating Kenichi Shawn
Tanzil's AI / machine-learning engineering work. They arrive from a link (résumé, profile,
message) and want to judge quickly what he has built and how he thinks _(inferred)_.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio. Success means a visitor can find selected work and notes within seconds,
read a project's problem → approach → contributions without friction, and reach a contact link.

## Positioning

Authored, quiet, and technical rather than templated. The character-field artwork and
the all-mono typography are the identity.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Routes: `/`, `/work/`, `/work/[slug]/`, `/notes/`, `/notes/[slug]/`, 404.
- Content lives in typed data files separate from components.
- Demo-content mode for review; it must not leak into a production build.
- Undecided: final project copy, notes, biography, contact links, and domain.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Kenichi Shawn Tanzil**. Role: **AI / Machine Learning Engineer**. These are the only confirmed identity facts.
- Dark aubergine palette and muted lavender/mauve accents, with no neon. Monospace everywhere (Iosevka).
- Abstract influences only: geometric discipline, stillness with organic movement, digital precision.
  No third-party characters, logos, or imagery.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. No projects, results, publications, employers, or testimonials have been supplied, and
none may be invented. Fixtures are labelled placeholders.

## Product Principles

1. Work first: the fastest path is name → selected work → a readable project page.
2. Never fabricate: empty is better than invented, and omitted is better than dead.
3. Texture serves character: the artwork adds personality but never blocks reading or navigation.
4. Calm by default, and the visitor stays in control of motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. The site must be fully usable with keyboard only, with JavaScript disabled, and with
`prefers-reduced-motion`.
